# 6-minute demo
1. Home: say what it is and that it is an educational prototype; point at the live status chips (model, device, latency are real).
2. Console, video: press Play. Explain boxes, track IDs, FPS (limited by the video's own frame rate) versus inference ms. Raise "Abstain below" to show UNCERTAIN boxes. Toggle "Also detect people and vehicles".
3. Webcam: press Webcam; show a printed sign or a sign image on a phone screen. Say honestly what it misses.
4. Event timeline: click an event, show its frame, export CSV, use "Signs only".
5. Perception Lab: same image with low light severity 4; show detections dropping.
6. Results: walk through detection table, confusion matrix, robustness curves (low light is worst), calibration (ECE before/after), latency. Say "single run, small German test set".
7. Signs page: show the registry with its verified/unverified flags and sources. Close with limitations and next steps (Indian signs).
